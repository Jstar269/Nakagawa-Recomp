Play your PSP games as native PC programs.

Nakagawa Recomp takes a PSP game you own and translates its code into a Windows program, once, before you play. It isn't an emulator, and it includes no games: you play your own copy.

Version 0.0.2 is an experimental pre-release. Hot Shots Tennis is playable, in Authentic or Enhanced mode, and most other games stop early.

You need Windows 11, a Vulkan graphics card, 8 GB of memory, 5 GB of free space, and a PSP game you own. The download includes the build tools, and no games or game data.

The download isn't code-signed, so Windows may warn you the first time. How to check your download.

How it works

  1. Add your game

    Extract the download, start bin\nakagawa_player.exe and drag in a disc image of a game you own. The player checks it and gets it ready, all on your PC.

  2. Build it

    Press BUILD PACKAGE. The player translates the game's code into C and compiles it into a Windows program. It takes a few minutes and needs no internet.

  3. Play

    Press PLAY to start the game, as released in Authentic mode, or in Enhanced mode with options such as Smooth motion and Widescreen. If it needs something Nakagawa can't do yet, it stops and tells you what's missing.
